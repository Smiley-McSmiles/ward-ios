Ward is a private messenger for the open XMPP network, built natively for iPhone and iPad.

Bring your own account and your own server. Ward speaks standard XMPP, so it works with Prosody, ejabberd, Conversations, Dino, Gajim, Monal and many other clients. There is no Ward account, no advertising and no analytics.

WHY WARD
• Your account, your server. Use any XMPP provider or host your own.
• One-to-one and group chats, channels, replies, reactions, edits and receipts.
• OMEMO 2 end-to-end encryption for one-to-one chats, with per-device sessions and verification.
• Photos, files, video, voice messages and GIF search.
• Markdown, code blocks, quotes, lists and tables rendered natively.
• Scheduled sending, chat export, global search and link previews.
• Server administration with every command your server advertises.
• Per-account XML console and service discovery for troubleshooting.
• Face ID or passcode app lock, screen privacy and Keychain-backed passwords.

BUILT FOR IOS
• Native SwiftUI with light, dark and nine accent colours.
• Responsive split view on iPad and a comfortable one-handed layout on iPhone.
• Spring animations, native sheets, context menus and swipe actions.
• Share sheet, Quick Look, Photos picker, camera and Files integration.
• Local notifications and unread badges while Ward is connected.

PRIVACY
Messages live on your device. One-to-one chats can use OMEMO 2 end-to-end encryption, so keys stay on your devices. Ward does not run a message server and does not collect your conversations. Your XMPP server can see metadata as with any XMPP client; use a server you trust or run your own. Link previews are optional and never include cookies or analytics. Read the privacy policy at the support link below.

OPEN SOURCE
Ward for iOS is MIT-licensed. The source is on GitHub, including the XMPP stack, the SQLite history store, the SwiftUI interface and the MIT-licensed OMEMO 2 implementation.

Ward is an independent client. XMPP is an open standard, and the XMPP name is used descriptively.
